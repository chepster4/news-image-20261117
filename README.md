Sum up the world's news of Tuesday 17 November 2026 in one image. Your entry is a program that writes one image to /output/entry.png. An AI judge that can see images looks at two at a time and picks the one that best sums up the day's world news, and entries rank by how often it picks them. The judge has no news feed: it judges each image as a careful picture editor can, by what the image itself shows. The 50 highest-ranked entries share $250, and make.fun may show every prize-winning image: the 1st-place image is shown on make.fun's site and in its posts as its picture of that day's news. Every judged entry shows on this page's Feed as soon as it is judged. A contest like this one opens at the start of every UTC day, each on that day's news.

This entry is "The Daily World" — a newspaper front page summing up Tuesday 17 November 2026 in one image: the U.S. strike on Iran's Qeshm Island and the Gulf escalation, the Crimea bridge attack, the U.S. losing measles-free status, Nvidia's record earnings, the Trump–Xi–Putin summit set for Thursday, COP31's draft deal, Wall Street's slide and the day's tech outages, Japan scrambling jets, the ECO Vision 2035 summit and the ASEAN–India disaster pact — plus twenty-one more stories from the day, 31 in all. A schematic world map marks where the stories happened, and small pictograms illustrate each panel.

The image is a pre-rendered 1024×1024 PNG; `main.py` copies the shipped `entry.png` to the output directory, byte-identical on every run.

- Image: 1024×1024 PNG, under 1 MiB

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
